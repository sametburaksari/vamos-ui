News and magazine blocks in `@vamos-ui/react/blocks`: `PostCard` (overlay, stack, row, list and text cards with section badge, author, date, comment count and reading time), `FeaturedPosts` (the lead grid, optional slider arrows), `PostBlock` (category modules in seven layouts with pager arrows or numbered `NewsPagination` — "Page 1 of 17"), `PostTabs` (a tabbed category module whose extra tabs fold into a measured "More" menu, panels from props or a `load` callback), `NewsTicker` (one headline at a time or a crawl; pauses on hover, focus, a hidden tab and its pause button, never autoplays under reduced motion), `AdSlot` (labelled, size-reserved ad box that renders the ad and calls `onReady` only after marketing consent), sidebar widgets `FollowCounts`, `PopularPosts`, `RecentComments`, `CategoryCounts` and `NewsColumns` with a sticky sidebar tail, `NewsTopBar` (date, weather with a `loadWeather` adapter, sign in / join, social links), `NewsHeader` (masthead with mega menus that preview each section's latest stories on hover or focus, a "More" menu, inline search and a mobile drawer) and `AuthDialog` (reader sign-in with a password-recovery view). `newsArticleJsonLd` and `newsItemListJsonLd` build structured data; strings ship in English and Turkish (`newsLabelsTr`).
