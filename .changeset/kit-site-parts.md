New components promoted from the example websites, generic and documented: `ReadingProgress` (progress through an article, with minutes left), `ModeToggle` (light / dark / system, persisted, with a no-flash head script), `SectionNav` (sticky in-page navigation that follows scroll), `TypingDemo` (scripted typing and rewrite demo; screen readers get the final text), `AudioPlayer` (chapters, skip, rate, transcript and download links, Media Session), `Countdown` (segments or inline, localized units, SSR-safe), `ImageHotspots` (selectable points on a picture: single or multiple, roving focus), `UptimeBars` (per-day status bars with legend and uptime %), `ReleaseNotes` (changelog with a version / date rail, change types and anchors), `FeedbackPrompt` ("Was this helpful?" with reasons and a comment), `FixtureList` and `StandingsTable` + `FormGuide` (fixtures, results and rankings for any head-to-head competition), `CapacityFinder` (options that fit a head count per arrangement) and `AccessibilityToolbar` (text size, token-safe high contrast, readable font, underlined links, reduced motion). `ActivityTimeline` gains an incident-updates example.
