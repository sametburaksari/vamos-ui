Site recipes. A recipe describes one kind of website as data: when it applies (site type, words in the name and description in English and Turkish, features, markets), its pages and, per page, the ordered section patterns (purpose, kit components with alternatives, the facts the business supplies, optional and conditional sections keyed to the interview: booking, shop, newsletter, several languages …), what the business must supply, looks that suit it (theme axes) and pitfalls. 34 recipes ship in `vamos-ui/project` (`RECIPES`, `pickRecipe`, `resolveRecipe`, `matchRecipes`). `vamos-ui new` picks one from the answers, keeps the sections that apply (`plan.blueprint`) and writes their components into the pages; the brand, content and build briefs carry the looks, the fields per section and the full blueprint. Plans no longer list starting templates (`plan.templates` is deprecated).
